What to look for in a maternity photographer

Start with portfolio consistency — does every gallery reflect a clear, cohesive style, or does quality vary widely? Look at how they handle light, how they pose and direct clients, and whether the images feel genuine or stiff. Read reviews specifically for how clients felt during the session, not just how the images turned out. The experience matters as much as the result.

When to schedule your maternity session

The sweet spot for most maternity sessions is between 28 and 34 weeks — the belly is beautifully round and prominent, but you're not yet in the final weeks of discomfort. If you're having twins or know you tend to deliver early, move the session earlier. Book at least 6–8 weeks in advance, as the best photographers in any market fill up quickly.

What to wear and how to prepare

Most photographers offer styling guidance as part of their experience. In general: flowing fabrics photograph beautifully, solid colors or simple patterns are more timeless than busy prints, and form-fitting options that showcase the bump work well for editorial looks. Bring your partner and any older children if you want them included. Relax — the best images come when you're not thinking about the camera.
